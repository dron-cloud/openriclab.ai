Dr. Ron's research primarily focuses on designing and building AI-Native FutureG Networked Systems spanning Open RAN, AI-RAN, and Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN)—bringing together AI models, programmable software-defined networking, experimental prototyping, and real-world 5G network data collected from 115 cells in Texas to enable intelligent systems that can sense environmental conditions, forecast future network states, and autonomously optimize networks performance toward the ambitious performance targets envisioned for 6G.

Public AI Research Platform

WirelessAI Chat: standards-grounded answers for wireless systems.

I am developing a public AI assistant that helps students, researchers, and engineers understand wireless systems through evidence-grounded explanations drawn from 3GPP, ITU, and O-RAN knowledge. The goal is not a generic chatbot, but a wireless engineering copilot that can reason across standards, explain concepts at different expertise levels, and return traceable technical references.

About

Research at the intersection of AI, wireless systems, and programmable networks.

I am an Assistant Research Professor in Electrical and Computer Engineering at NC State University. My work focuses on Open RAN, AI-RAN, trustworthy wireless intelligence, and LEO/NTN space networks.

A consistent theme across my research is moving beyond standalone algorithms toward systems that can be measured, deployed, controlled, and experimentally evaluated under realistic network conditions.
